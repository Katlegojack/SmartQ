Vision

Make waiting predictable, transparent and fair.

Smart Q aims to transform the way people experience queues by making waiting times predictable, transparent, and fair. The platform enables customers to book services, track their position in real time, receive accurate waiting-time predictions, and avoid spending unnecessary hours in physical queues.

Mission

Give people their time back.

Make queues predictable, transparent, fair, and continuously improving.
